# Later reads can displace previously complete evidence

The existing 32,000-character context projection preserves complete annotated
read evidence for **1/3, 2/3 and 0/3 tasks** after one, three and five reads. Before
projection, cumulative read delivery was **1/3, 2/3 and 2/3**. More reads therefore
reduce retained evidence in this fixed original-question development diagnostic.

The [plan](plan.md), [protocol](protocol.json), [summary](summary.json) and
[receipt](receipt.json) preserve the result. Implementation `e082698`, freeze
`a08780c`. The original reading runtime still matches its historical protocol;
complete saved results and journals replayed exactly without field substitution.
Fifteen verification reads and six verification searches occurred; no model calls.

All nine snapshots project successfully; none raises the context-size stop.
At one and three reads, no read response is omitted. At five reads:

- NIKE loses complete coverage of every annotated role: statement identity,
  period/scale, operating, investing and financing totals. Earlier reads had
  supplied a complete union at three reads.
- Ulta retains its annual-period heading but loses complete coverage of the four
  SG&A comparison/driver/offset roles. Its first read alone had the whole packet.
- Walmart remains incomplete; its original retrieval already missed the required
  statement evidence. Context retention does not explain that earlier failure.

The policy protects the latest search and latest read, then adds older results
newest-first if they fit. This can favor later, lower-ranked passages over earlier
useful evidence. It omits whole responses, preserves canonical history and exact
retained strings, and respects its size limit; the failure is evidence retention,
not clipping or corruption. No production policy was changed during measurement.

## Scope and next decision

These are independent synthetic histories constructed from one actual search
response and saved ordered reads, with graph-exact output serialization. They
are not observed model trajectories. No assistant reasoning, model inference or
later recovery search is simulated. Omission notices grant no read coverage even
when they retain locators. Search snippets are not pooled into the read metric.

Counts use serialized tool-output-string characters, including escaping. They are
not tokens, prompt-wide character limits or the prior report's JSON-byte measure.
The unchanged posthoc packets and Ulta fiscal-year convention limitation remain.
Human financial validation and unseen generalization are not established.

A future challenger should test evidence retention under the same character budget
and required-latest-result constraints, using a declared label-free priority rule.
It must preserve this negative baseline and record any losses, blocked projections
or extra tool cost. Increasing the limit or claiming that more delivered text is
necessarily better would not resolve the measured selection problem.
