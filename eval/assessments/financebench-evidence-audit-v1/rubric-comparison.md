1. Same fact/entity/period/unit/metric/attribution/source restriction at another

2. Added corroboration or stricter obligations, such as a currency legend,
   exhaustive definition, valuation policy or aggregation link.
3. Changed interpretation, such as a different numerator, denominator, adjustment,
   averaging convention or classification rule.
